Relay

Stream your Chromebook screen directly to Twitch from your browser

Using Relay

1. Open Relay in Chrome
2. Get your Twitch stream key from your Twitch dashboard
3. Paste your stream key into Relay
4. Click Share screen and choose your screen window or tab
5. Click Go live
6. When you’re finished click Stop

Good to know

Keep the Relay tab open while streaming. Closing the tab will end your stream

For the most reliable audio sharing select a Chrome tab and enable Share tab audio

Your stream key is only kept in the page’s memory for that session and isn’t stored on a server
